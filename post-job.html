Post a job

Posting is free. Verified local freelancers will send you proposals.

What happens next

1. Freelancers send proposals with a price and a timeframe.

2. You pick one and pay through PayPal. The money is held, not paid out.

3. When you approve the work, the payment is released.

You pay no fee. The flat 8% fee comes out of the freelancer's payout.
